# Isolated Clouds

## Market Share by Vendor

1. Amazon Web Services (AWS)
2. Microsoft Azure (Azure)
3. Google Cloud Platform (GCP)
4. Oracle Cloud Infrastructure (OCI)

 
Exact vendor-by-vendor revenue figures for classified Top Secret and Secret environments are not publicly disclosed due to national security secrecy. However, industry analyses from firms like IDC and Mordor Intelligence show that Amazon Web Services (AWS) holds the commanding market lead, followed by Microsoft Azure in second place, with Google Cloud and Oracle rapidly expanding their footprints as challengers.

Instead of open commercial market share, the Department of Defense (DoD) and Intelligence Community (IC) divide this market through **multi-award enterprise contracts**—primarily the $9 billion **JWCC** (**J**oint **W**arfighting **C**loud **C**apability) for the DoD and the **C2E** (**C**ommercial **C**loud **E**nterprise) for the IC.

- **Amazon Web Services** (Dominant Incumbent). AWS holds the largest installed base across both DoD and IC classified networks. It secured a decade-long head start with the CIA's original $600 million **C2S** contract in 2013 and continues to lead via dedicated, physically isolated Secret and Top Secret regions, alongside newer offerings like AWS Secret Cloud for Industry for defense contractors.
- **Microsoft Azure** (Primary Challenger). Microsoft commands the second-largest share, heavily anchored by its ubiquitous Office 365 Government Community Cloud (GCC) footprint and DoD Impact Level 6 (IL6) Azure Government Secret regions. It has gained significant share in classified AI workloads by deploying IL6-authorized Azure OpenAI models.
- **Google Cloud** (Fast-Growing AI & Edge Contender). Historically trailing AWS and Microsoft in defense, Google has accelerated its share by winning major task orders under JWCC (such as the Navy's Neptune cloud program and DARPA R&D initiatives). Agencies increasingly select Google for **specialized AI**, **TPU compute**, **BigQuery analytics**, and air-gapped **tactical edge** hardware.
- **Oracle Cloud Infrastructure** (Specialized Database & Sovereign Niche). OCI holds the smallest share among the four primary JWCC hyperscalers, though it maintains a strong niche footprint. Its adoption is driven by agencies migrating legacy on-premises Oracle databases and high-volume relational workloads into classified cloud regions.

### Job Postings for cleared DoD/IC contracts

1. AWS: 55%-65%
2. Azure: 25%-35%
3. GCP + OCI: 10%-15%


#### Key Hiring Patterns in Cleared Environments

- **Multi-Cloud Generalists Are Preferred**. Defense contractors frequently list "experience with AWS, Azure, GCP, or OCI" together in a single job description. As one consensus on Reddit notes, holding certifications in less-saturated platforms like GCP or OCI alongside a primary AWS credential helps cleared engineers stand out for multi-cloud DoD contracts.
- **Vendor-Agnostic Skills Matter Most**. Across all four vendors, cleared job postings consistently prioritize **Kubernetes**, **Terraform** (IaC), **CI/CD pipelines**, and **DoD 8570/8140 compliance** (such as **CompTIA Security+** or **CISSP**) over deep expertise in proprietary cloud services.
