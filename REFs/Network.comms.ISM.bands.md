# ISM (**I**ndustrial, **S**cientific, and **M**edical) / U-NII (**U**nlicensed **N**ational **I**nformation **I**nfrastructure) 

Bands that carry data, IoT signals, and short-range links:

- **13.56 MHz** (**HiFER** / **HF ISM**):   
    Used for **RFID**, **NFC**, and low-power experimental transmissions.
- **49 MHz** ("**49ers**" band):  
    Used for older **cordless phones**, **baby monitors**, walkie-talkies, and low-power home-built or remote-control devices.
- **900 MHz** (902–928 **MHz ISM**):   
    Long-range IoT (such as **LoRa**, **Zigbee**, and **Z-Wave**), cordless phones, and industrial wireless links.
- **2.4 GHz** (2.400–2.4835 **GHz ISM**):   
    Widely used for **Wi-Fi**, **Bluetooth**, Bluetooth Low Energy (**BLE**), **Zigbee**, and **cordless** peripherals.
- **5 GHz** (**U-NII-1** through **U-NII-4**):   
    Higher-speed Wi-Fi and fixed wireless broadband, often using Dynamic Frequency Selection (DFS) to avoid radar.
- **6 GHz** (5.925–7.125 GHz, **U-NII-5** through **U-NII-8**):   
    Used for Wi-Fi 6E and Wi-Fi 7 across Low Power Indoor (**LPI**), Very Low Power (**VLP**), and Automated Frequency Coordination (**AFC**) modes.
- **24 GHz** & **60 GHz** (**Millimeter Wave** / **WiGig**):  
    Short-range, high-capacity wireless links, radar/sensing, and point-to-point connections.
